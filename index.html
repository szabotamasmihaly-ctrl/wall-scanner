Checking whether this phone supports live AR…
How it works
  1. While building: open a new wall, point at its two bottom corners, then film the wall slowly while pipes, cables and rebar are still visible. Mark each one on the screen.
  2. After plastering: open the wall, point at the same two corners again. The saved picture and marks appear on the finished wall.
  3. Aim at a spot to see how far the nearest pipe or cable is, and how much free space you have around it.
Tap the picture to see distances.
